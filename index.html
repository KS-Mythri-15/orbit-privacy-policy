Orbit – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Orbit is a Zero-Trust personal assistant developed as an academic project.

Information We Access

When users connect their Google account, Orbit accesses the Google Calendar information permitted by the user.

Purpose of Access

Google Calendar access is used to create and retrieve calendar events requested by the user.

Data Storage and Security

Orbit uses authentication, authorization checks and encrypted storage for sensitive information.

Data Sharing

Orbit does not sell personal information. Data is processed to provide the application's intended functionality and connected services.

User Control

Users can revoke Google account access through their Google Account security settings.
